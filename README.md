# GuessT..!

**Guess + T** — the T is whatever you want it to be:

> Time, Turn, Touch, Tilt, Tap, Team, Try, Task, Twist, ...


A fast, chaotic, phone-on-your-forehead party game — the kind that turns a quiet room into a shouting match in about ten seconds. No
setup, no learning curve: pick a mode, pick a category, and start
yelling clues at whoever's holding the phone.

## How it works

Stick the phone to your forehead, screen facing your friends. They
throw clues at you. Get it right, tilt the phone down. Stuck? Tilt up
to pass. The clock is always running, and it's always louder than you
expect.

## Pick your GT Mode

**⭐ Classic** — The one everyone starts with. 60 seconds, passing
allowed, straightforward fun. Built for a first game night.

**⏱️ Time Attack** — 30 seconds. No passing. No mercy. Every word counts
and the clock feels like it's cheating.

**👥 Teams** — Split into two or more squads, take turns, stack up the
points. Built for the whole party, not just the two people closest to
the phone.

**👶 Kids Mode** — Animals, colors, simple objects — 90 relaxed seconds
built for younger players and family game nights, not just the sharpest
guessers in the room.

**🔥 Hard Mode** — History, science, culture. Less time, and passing
actually costs you. This one's for the people who think Classic was too
easy.

**🛠️ Custom Mode** — Write your own words, save your own deck, and play
it like Classic. Inside jokes, work drama, whatever — if you can type
it, you can play it.

## Built for the moment, not the menu

- Every mode explains itself before you play — no rules to memorize
  mid-game
- Tilt-based scoring with sound and haptic feedback on every guess
- Word categories you actually pick, not ones picked for you
- A results screen at the end of every round: hits, passes, bragging
  rights
